Privacy Policy

The Ghost Gauge app for iPhone and iPad

Last updated 27 November 2026

Your readings, rides and locations stay on your device. Without an account, the app talks to nothing but your gauge and our firmware site. The leaderboard is optional: if you make an account, we keep what you post to it, described below, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

On your device only

Readings from your gauge

What your Can-Am reports to your Ghost Gauge, such as engine speed, boost, temperatures, voltage, road speed and gear. It arrives over Bluetooth, is shown on screen, and is written to your ride files. It is never sent to us.

Rides, runs and the service log

While the engine runs, the app writes one sample a second to a ride file in its own storage. The last 50 rides are kept. Timed runs and your service log are kept the same way. You can delete any ride from the ride list.

Location

Map each ride (Settings › This iPhone) is on unless you turn it off. While a ride is being recorded, the app saves your position with each sample so the ride can be drawn on a map, including with your phone locked, which is why it asks to use location in the background. Positions come from your phone, or from your gauge's own GPS when it has one, and stay in the ride file on your device. Turn the switch off, or refuse location, and the rest of the app works without it.

Camera and Photos

Only when you share a ride: the camera is used if you take a photo with the ride's sticker on it, and a photo is read only if you pick one. Saving adds to your library; the app asks only for permission to add, not to read it. Sharing goes through the iOS share sheet, to whatever you choose.

Notifications

Alarms and the Lock Screen reading are made on your device from your gauge's readings.

The leaderboard, if you make an account

The leaderboard is optional. Everything else in the app works without an account.

What we keep

  • How you sign in: your phone number, or the identifier Apple gives us for Sign in with Apple. The app asks Apple for neither your name nor your email.
  • Your profile: your driver name, country, profile picture if you add one, your build sheet (model, year and modifications) and the serial number of the gauge you claim.
  • Runs you post: the times, and the gauge's signed record of the run (speed, altitude and time from its GPS, but no positions), so we can check it.
  • What you do there: the drivers you follow, your crew, and any run you report.
  • Screens from your garage: screens a garage sends to your Pro are kept for your account until you install them.
  • Your phone and gauge: the phone model, iOS and app version, language, region and time zone, whether notifications and location are allowed, a push token so we can tell you about the monthly shootout and new screens, how many rides and runs the app keeps (counts only, not the rides), and the connected gauge's serial, firmware and which Can-Am model it reads. This helps us support you.
  • Sign-in requests: the IP address a phone sign-in came from, to slow down abuse.

Who sees it

Your driver name, country, picture, build, posted runs and crew are shown to other drivers. Untick Show me on public boards when you sign up to stay off the public boards. Your phone number, device details and the drivers you report are never shown to anyone else. Ghost Electronics can see everything listed above, to run the leaderboard and support you.

Where it is kept

On a server Ghost Electronics rents from Hostinger, in Malaysia, at api.ghost.com.qa. Push notifications go through Apple. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting it

Open your profile and tap Delete account. Your account and everything you posted are removed for good, and your Sign in with Apple link is revoked at Apple.

Firmware updates

Now and then the app checks ghost.com.qa for new firmware for your gauge and downloads it from GitHub Pages. These downloads carry no identifier, readings or location, though like any web request they reach GitHub from your IP address. The firmware then goes to your gauge over Bluetooth, or on older Standard and Nano firmware over the gauge's own Wi-Fi, which has no internet connection. Turn off Download firmware updates in Settings › This iPhone to stop the automatic downloads.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. There is no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK in the app. If you share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass on crash reports, which hold no readings, rides or locations.

Children

Ghost Gauge is a tool for a vehicle accessory and is not directed at children.
