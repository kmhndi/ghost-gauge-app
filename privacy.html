GHOST GAUGE SUPPORT PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Ghost Gauge for iPhone and iPad

Last updated 18 September 2026

Ghost Gauge has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything the app records stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us, because there is nowhere to send it — the app talks only to your own gauge.

What the app handles

Readings from your gauge

Belt and coolant temperature, intake air temperature, boost, oil pressure, battery voltage, engine speed and road speed arrive over Bluetooth from the Ghost Gauge hardware. They are shown on screen and, if ride logging is on, written to a file on your device.

Ride logs

With ride logging on, the app writes one sample a second to a file in its own storage while the gauge is connected — about 94 KB an hour. The most recent 50 rides are kept and older ones are removed automatically. You can delete any ride, or all of them, in the app.

Location

If you turn on position logging, the app records your location alongside each ride sample so a ride can be drawn on a map. This happens only while a ride is being recorded, including when your phone is locked and in your pocket, which is why the app asks for background location. Positions are written into the same ride file on your device and are never transmitted. Turn it off in Settings › This iPhone, or deny location permission entirely — the rest of the app works without it.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used to find and talk to your gauge, and to stay connected in the background so alarms still reach you with the phone locked. The app scans only for the Ghost Gauge service and does not connect to other devices.

Local network

When you update the gauge's firmware, the app joins the gauge's own temporary Wi-Fi network and sends the firmware file to it directly. Nothing is uploaded to the internet, and the gauge's network provides no internet connection.

Notifications and Live Activities

Alarm notifications and the Lock Screen reading are produced on your device from readings your gauge sent. No push server is involved.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data. The developer has no access to your readings, ride logs or location. There is no tracking, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK in the app.

Deleting your data

Delete individual rides from the ride list in the app, or delete the app from your device to remove everything it stored, including all ride files and settings.

Children

Ghost Gauge is a tool for a vehicle accessory and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.
