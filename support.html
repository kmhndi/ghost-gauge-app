The Ghost Gauge app for iPhone and iPad

The Ghost Gauge app goes with the Ghost Gauge Pro, the Ghost Gauge Standard and the Ghost Gauge Nano, which read your Can-Am's own data. It shows your readings live, warns you when one passes its limit, records your rides, times your runs, installs your garage's screens on a Pro, keeps your service log, and keeps your gauge's firmware up to date.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later
  • A Ghost Gauge Pro, Standard or Nano fitted to your Can-Am, with the key on

Connecting for the first time

  1. Turn the key on and keep your phone near the gauge.
  2. Open the app and pick your gauge from the list. Its name ends in the gauge's own four-character code, for example GHOST-GAUGE-8GSH.
  3. The gauge shows a six-digit code and iOS asks for it. Type it in. After that your phone is remembered and connects by itself.

Common problems

The app keeps looking for the gauge

  • Check the key is on. A Nano goes to sleep two minutes after the Can-Am is switched off and wakes when it starts again.
  • Check Bluetooth is on, and that the app is allowed to use it in iOS Settings › Ghost Gauge.
  • Don't swipe the app away in the app switcher. That stops it connecting by itself.
  • If the gauge you used before is gone, tap Choose a different gauge on the connect screen.

iOS asks for a code the gauge never shows

The gauge has forgotten this phone, but the phone still remembers the gauge. Go to iOS Settings › Bluetooth and forget your gauge (GHOST-GAUGE- and its code). Then open the app and tap PAIR AGAIN.

The app says VEHICLE NOT CONNECTED

The gauge is on and talking to your phone, but it hears nothing from your Can-Am. Check the key is on and the gauge's connector is pushed fully home. A reading your Can-Am doesn't report shows as --. The gauge never makes a number up.

A setting won't save

If Settings says the gauge didn't answer, tap Retry once the gauge is back in range.

A firmware update stopped part way

Keep the app open on the update screen, with your phone near the gauge, until it finishes. Updates go over Bluetooth. If one fails, the gauge keeps running the firmware it already had: a failed update cannot leave it without one. Tap Try again.

A Standard or Nano on older firmware updates over the gauge's own Wi-Fi instead. If it can't reach the gauge, check Local Network is on for Ghost Gauge in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. The gauge goes back to Bluetooth by itself if the update doesn't arrive.

My garage's screens haven't arrived

Screens your garage sends to your Pro go to your account. Sign in with the same account that claimed the Pro, connect to the Pro, and the app offers to install them.

Rides aren't being recorded

A ride is recorded while the engine is running. Five minutes with the engine off ends it, and a ride under a minute is not kept.

Your leaderboard account

  • Signing up: use Sign in with Apple or your phone number, then claim your gauge. Only the gauge your phone is connected to can be claimed.
  • Selling your gauge or your Can-Am: open your profile and tap Release on your gauge. Its next owner can then claim it from their app. Runs you already posted stay up.
  • Deleting your account: open your profile and tap Delete account. Your account and everything you posted are removed for good.
