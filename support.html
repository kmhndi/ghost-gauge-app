Ghost Gauge for iPhone and iPad

Ghost Gauge is the companion app for the Ghost Gauge dash cluster, a custom gauge for the Can-Am Maverick X3. It shows the gauge's readings, keeps alarms on your Lock Screen, records rides, changes every setting on the panel, and installs new firmware.

No gauge? Turn on Demo mode in Settings › This iPhone — or wait a few seconds on the connect screen and tap Try demo mode. The app then runs against a simulated gauge so you can see everything it does.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later
  • A Ghost Gauge cluster, powered on and running Bluetooth (its Telemetry screen shows BLUETOOTH NAME — GHOST-GAUGE)

Connecting for the first time

  1. Power the gauge and keep the phone near it.
  2. Open the app. It finds the gauge by itself.
  3. The first time you change a setting, the gauge shows a six-digit code and iOS asks for it. Type it in. After that the phone is remembered and reconnects without asking.

Common problems

The app says it is looking for the gauge and never finds it

  • Check the gauge's Telemetry screen. If it shows Wi-Fi rather than Bluetooth, it is mid-update: it returns to Bluetooth by itself within five minutes, or you can power it off and on.
  • Check Bluetooth is on, and that Ghost Gauge is allowed to use it in iOS Settings › Ghost Gauge.
  • Don't swipe the app away in the app switcher — that stops it reconnecting on its own.

It asks for a pairing code that the gauge never shows

The gauge has probably forgotten this phone while the phone still remembers the gauge. Forget GHOST-GAUGE in iOS Settings › Bluetooth, then open the app and pair again.

Settings won't save

Changes need a paired phone. If the status bar at the bottom of Settings says the gauge didn't answer, tap Retry once the gauge is back in range.

A firmware update stopped part way

The app puts the gauge back on Bluetooth when an update fails. If it somehow stays on Wi-Fi, the gauge gives up and returns to Bluetooth by itself after five minutes — or power it off and on. The gauge keeps running the firmware it already had; a failed update cannot leave it without one.

Rides aren't being recorded

Check Settings › Ride log shows the log running. Recording starts when readings arrive and a break longer than five minutes begins a new ride; rides under a minute are discarded.

On the gauge itself

  • Tap the right half of the panel for the next screen, the left half for the previous.
  • Hold the left half until the bar fills, then let go, to step the colours: dark → light → automatic. The screen tells you which one it is about to switch to, and letting go before the bar fills cancels.
  • Hold the right half the same way to reboot it.
